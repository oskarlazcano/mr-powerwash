Benefits of Professional Pressure Washing

Regular cleaning isn't just about looks. It protects your property and what you've invested in it.

Boosts curb appeal and value

A clean house and driveway make a strong first impression, especially before a sale.

Protects from deterioration

Mildew, algae and grime break down surfaces over time. Removing them makes them last longer.

Removes stains and buildup

Oil, rust, dirt and weather stains come out of concrete that regular hosing won't touch.

Preps for paint and stain

Paint and sealant stick better to clean surfaces, so the finish lasts longer.

Safer and healthier

Slippery algae on walkways and mold on siding are hazards. Washing removes them.

Saves you the weekend

Skip renting equipment and spending a Saturday on it. We bring the gear and get it done.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water for hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solutions, which is safer for siding, stucco, brick and painted surfaces.
